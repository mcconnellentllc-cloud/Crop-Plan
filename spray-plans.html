Corn Herbicide Spray Programs

Understanding 2-Pass vs 3-Pass Weed Control

Why Spray Program Selection Matters

Research from the University of Missouri found that in 67% of trials, the highest corn yields were obtained with a two-pass program (pre-emergence + post-emergence). A comprehensive two-pass weed control program can return $720/acre compared to $540/acre for a one-pass pre-emergence only program.

Resistance Management

Multiple modes of action reduce herbicide-resistant weed development

Extended Control

Residual herbicides control late-emerging weeds like Palmer amaranth

Flexibility

Pre-emergence provides insurance if post applications are delayed

Herbicide Guide - What Each Chemical Does

Herbicide Active Ingredient Mode of Action What It Controls
Glyphosate Glyphosate (41%) Group 9 (EPSPS inhibitor) Burndown of emerged grasses and broadleaves. Essential for no-till systems.
Atrazine 4L Atrazine Group 5 (Photosystem II inhibitor) Broadleaf weeds, small-seeded annuals. Residual activity on pigweeds, kochia, lambsquarters.
Valor SX Flumioxazin Group 14 (PPO inhibitor) Pre-emergence broadleaf control. Excellent on waterhemp, Palmer amaranth, morningglory.
Dual II Magnum S-metolachlor Group 15 (Long-chain fatty acid inhibitor) Annual grasses, yellow nutsedge, pigweeds. Extended residual grass control.
Warrant Acetochlor Group 15 (Long-chain fatty acid inhibitor) Residual grass control. Can be applied post-emergence for extended activity.
DiFlexx Dicamba Group 4 (Synthetic auxin) Broadleaf weeds including kochia, marestail, velvetleaf. Post-emergence activity.
Starane Ultra Fluroxypyr Group 4 (Synthetic auxin) Broadleaf weeds. Excellent on bindweed, volunteer sunflower, kochia.
AMS Ammonium Sulfate Adjuvant Water conditioner. Improves glyphosate performance in hard water.
Hydrovant Surfactant blend Adjuvant Improves spray coverage and herbicide uptake. Reduces drift.

Recommendation

For most fields: Use the 2-pass program. It provides excellent weed control at a reasonable cost and has been proven to maximize yields in 67% of research trials.

Consider 3-pass when:

  • Field has heavy winter annual pressure (marestail, henbit)
  • History of herbicide-resistant weeds
  • Late planting where weeds have gotten ahead
  • No-till with heavy residue requiring early burndown
