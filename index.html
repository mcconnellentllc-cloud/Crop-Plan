How This Flex Lease Works

  1. Shared Input Costs: Landowner contributes 25% of all input costs (seed, fertilizer, chemicals, operations) excluding the base rent.
  2. Base Rent: Tenant pays a reduced base rent of $50/acre to ensure landowner receives minimum income.
  3. Revenue at Breakeven: If revenue just covers costs, tenant receives 80% of revenue and landowner receives 20%.
  4. Profit Sharing: After all costs are covered, profits are split 55% to tenant and 45% to landowner.
  5. Risk Sharing: In poor years, both parties share the loss proportionally. In good years, both benefit from higher prices and yields.

Flex lease calculations based on Iowa State University Extension guidelines and common crop-share arrangements in Northeast Colorado.
